JEE Main Mock Test Instructions

General Instructions:

  1. The test consists of 90 questions across 3 sections: Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
  2. Total duration of the examination is 180 minutes. The timer starts when you click "Start Exam".
  3. The clock is set at the server and the countdown timer at the top right corner of screen will display the remaining time.
  4. Each section has 20 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) and 10 Numerical Value Questions. You have to attempt any 5 out of 10 Numerical questions.
  5. Marking Scheme: +4 for a correct answer, -1 for an incorrect answer (MCQs only), 0 for unattempted or incorrect numerical questions.

Navigating the Questions:

You can click on the question number in the palette on the right to go to that question directly.
